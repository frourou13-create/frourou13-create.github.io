Agence Publication

Private publishing tool for my own YouTube channels

Privacy PolicyTerms of UseContact

Agence Publication is a private Windows desktop application, used only by its owner (Fedi Rourou), to upload, schedule and follow his own short-form and long-form videos on his own YouTube channels.

The application runs locally on the owner's computer. It is not distributed or sold, and no other user signs in.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's finished videos to his own channels with their title, description, tags, category, language, thumbnail and subtitles, and schedules their publication time.
  • Shows the names of the owner's channels and the view and like counts of the owner's own videos.
  • Never accesses, collects or stores data about other channels or other users.

This application uses YouTube API Services.
